Point de Rosée is a French-language YouTube channel of short science explainers: youtube.com/@pointderoseeTV.

Point de Rosée publication is the private tool the channel owner uses to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It runs only on the owner's computer and has no other users.
